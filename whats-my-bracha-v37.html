Duration · Kdei Achilas Pras
What is this? A kzayit must be eaten within kdei achilas pras — the time it takes to eat half a loaf of bread. If you stretch your kzayit beyond that window, the after-blessing doesn't apply. Opinions on the exact duration range from ~2 to ~9 minutes.
